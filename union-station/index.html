Water damage risks in Union Station

  • Multi-floor water migration. Leaks from upper units travel through ceilings and wall assemblies, damaging several homes from a single source.
  • Appliance and fixture failures. Dishwashers, washing machines, ice makers, and water heaters in condos and lofts are frequent leak sources.
  • Shared plumbing stacks. Drain and supply lines serving many units see constant demand, and a single failure can spread across floors.
  • Storm intrusions. Wind-driven rain can exploit window flashing and rooftop assemblies, sending water into top-floor walls and ceilings.
  • Frozen pipes in winter. Pipes in parking levels, exterior walls, and unoccupied units can freeze during extended cold snaps.
