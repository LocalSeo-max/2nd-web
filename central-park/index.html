Water damage risks in Central Park

  • Appliance and fixture failures. Newer homes have more water-using appliances — dishwashers, ice makers, second-floor laundry — and each connection is a potential leak point.
  • Water heater ruptures. Tank-style heaters typically last around a decade; when the tank fails it can release dozens of gallons at once, often into a finished lower level.
  • Irrigation and sprinkler line breaks. Sprinkler heads and lateral lines damaged by landscaping or freezing can flood yards and seep toward foundations.
  • Storm-driven roof and flashing leaks. High winds and hail can lift shingles and compromise flashing, letting water into attics and wall cavities.
  • Grading and drainage problems. If soil settles or landscaping directs runoff toward the house, heavy rain can pool at the foundation and push moisture into the lower level.
