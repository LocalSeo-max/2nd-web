What Rapid Water Damage Pros Is

Rapid Water Damage Pros is a referral service for the Denver metro area. We are not a restoration company ourselves — we don't send our own crews, equipment, or trucks to your property. Instead, when you call us, we connect you with an independent local water damage restoration provider that serves your area.

Water damage is stressful and often urgent. A burst pipe, a flooded basement, or storm water intrusion can't wait for business hours. Our role is to make the first step easy: one call reaches our referral line, and we put you in touch with a local provider that can assess your situation and handle the restoration work. That includes water damage restoration in Denver and the surrounding communities.

How It Works

  1. You call. Reach us any time at (303) 279-8078. Describe what's happening — where the water is coming from, how much there is, and how urgent it feels.
  2. We connect you with an independent local Denver provider. We refer your call to an independent water damage restoration provider serving your part of the metro area.
  3. The provider assesses and restores. The provider evaluates the damage, explains their recommended plan, and — if you choose to hire them — carries out the extraction, drying, and restoration work. Your service agreement is directly with that provider, not with us.

Because we focus on connecting you quickly rather than performing the work, you skip the search, the hold music, and the guesswork of figuring out which company actually serves your neighborhood.

A Clear Distinction: Referral vs. Provider

It's important to be clear about the difference between our role and the provider's role:

  • Rapid Water Damage Pros operates the referral line. We take your call and connect you with an independent local provider.
  • The independent provider performs all restoration services — water extraction, drying, mold remediation, repairs, and anything else needed at your property.

We do not perform restoration work, issue quotes for the work, or guarantee a provider's availability, pricing, or results. Any contract for services is between you and the provider you choose. We also never invent or publish provider credentials, licenses, ratings, or reviews on their behalf — what you see from a provider is what the provider represents about itself.

Our Service Area

We serve the Denver metro area, including neighborhoods across the city and nearby communities. Whether you're dealing with emergency water damage, a flooded basement, or moisture that may need mold remediation, one call connects you to a local provider ready to help.

Not sure if your area is covered? Contact us — our service area covers the greater Denver metro, and we'll point you in the right direction.

What to Expect When You Call

Calling a referral line is simple and pressure-free:

  • Someone answers quickly. Water damage doesn't keep office hours, so our line is open around the clock.
  • We'll ask a few practical questions — your location, the source of the water (if you know it), and whether it's an active leak — so we can route your call to an appropriate local provider.
  • You'll be connected with an independent provider serving your area. From there, the provider takes over: they'll assess the situation and discuss next steps directly with you.

There's no obligation. You decide whether to move forward with any provider we connect you with.
