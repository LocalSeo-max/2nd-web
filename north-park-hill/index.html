Water damage risks in North Park Hill

  • Frozen and burst pipes. Extended freezes can split pipes in unheated spaces; the resulting flood often isn't discovered until the thaw, after water has run for hours.
  • Aging plumbing systems. Long-serving supply and drain lines face the general risks of corrosion and joint fatigue — a Denver-wide concern in established housing.
  • Spring snowmelt seepage. Rapid March and April melt pushes groundwater through foundation cracks and floor joints into basements.
  • Roof and flashing leaks. Heavy snow loads and summer storms exploit worn roofing, sending water into attics and down interior walls.
  • Appliance failures. Water heaters, washing machines, and supply hoses fail without regard to season and can flood a home in minutes.
