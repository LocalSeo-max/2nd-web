Water damage risks in Highland

  • Sewer backups during heavy rain. Intense storms can surcharge older sewer laterals and drains, pushing contaminated water up through basement floor drains and lower-level fixtures.
  • Aging supply and drain lines. Decades of service bring the general risks of corrosion, scale buildup, and joint failure — a common Denver-wide pattern in older housing stock.
  • Basement flooding and seepage. Spring snowmelt and summer downpours push groundwater through settled foundations into basements and lower levels.
  • Winter pipe freezes. Pipes in crawlspaces, basements, and exterior walls can freeze and burst during prolonged cold.
  • Roof and flashing wear. Years of hail, wind, and snow load degrade roofing and flashing, opening paths for stormwater into attics and walls.
