Water damage risks in Hampden South

  • Finished-basement flooding. Finished lower levels multiply the damage from any water event — drywall, carpet, furniture, and electronics all soak up water quickly.
  • Sump pump failures. Pumps that fail during storms or power outages leave basements defenseless against rising groundwater.
  • Spring snowmelt seepage. March and April melt pushes groundwater against foundations, finding its way through floor cracks and wall joints.
  • Summer storms and drainage. Heavy rain can overwhelm gutters, downspouts, and yard grading, sending water into window wells and against foundation walls.
  • Appliance and plumbing leaks. Water heaters, washing machines, and supply lines fail without warning — a general risk in suburban housing of any age.
