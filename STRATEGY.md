# SEO & Content Strategy — Rapid Water Damage Pros
**Domain:** https://rapidwaterdamagedenver.com · **Market:** Denver, CO, USA · **Model:** Pay-per-call referral service · **Built:** 2026-09-28

## 1. SEO / Content Strategy

**Positioning.** The homepage is the primary topical and commercial authority page for "Water Damage Restoration in Denver, CO". It is deliberately the richest page on the site (~2,550 words): direct-answer definition, transparent referral-model section, all 30 services grouped by intent, Denver-specific problem scenarios, all 30 neighborhoods, urgency/mold-timeline guidance, 10 FAQs, and repeated conversion CTAs. Supporting pages exist to (a) capture long-tail service + city and service + neighborhood queries, and (b) push contextual authority back to the homepage through natural paragraph links with varied anchor text — never relying on nav/cards/footer alone.

**Keyword philosophy.** Genuine search intent over volume. No search-volume numbers are claimed anywhere (none were verified). Targets are built from observed SERP patterns in this niche: ranking Denver pages consistently use emergency framing, a 5-step process (inspection → extraction → drying/dehumidification → cleaning → restoration), 24/7 emphasis, and service-by-cause organization. Content answers the question first, then expands — optimized for both classic results and AI Overviews (definitions, question-based H2s, lists, entity relationships between service ↔ cause ↔ location).

**Conversion design (pay-per-call).** Every page carries: header click-to-call, sticky mobile call bar ("Call Now for Fast Service 24/7 – (303) 279-8078"), in-content tel links, and a final CTA band. Water-damage pay-per-call is an emergency vertical — speed and availability messaging ("Calls answered 24/7", "Free to call — no obligation") outranks feature lists. No contact form: calls are the product.

**Trust without fabrication.** The site is a referral service, stated plainly on the homepage ("How Our Referral Service Works"), about page, and in the footer of every page. Zero invented reviews, ratings, licenses, certifications, awards, prices, statistics, or years in business — anywhere. Provider capability is described only in general, attributable terms ("independent local providers").

**Seasonal intent coverage.** Winter: frozen/burst pipe pages + prevention post. Spring: snowmelt/basement seepage content. Summer: storm/roof-leak content. Year-round: appliance leaks, sewage, mold.

## 2. Site Architecture

```
/ (homepage — authority hub)
├── /{service-slug}/ × 30        (commercial service pages)
├── /{neighborhood-slug}/ × 30   (local pages, ZIP in title/H1)
├── /about/ /contact/
├── /blog/ + /blog/{post-slug}/ × 8
├── /404.html, /robots.txt, /sitemap.xml
└── /assets/{css,js,img}/
```

Flat first-level URLs (no /services/ or /locations/ prefixes). Internal linking hierarchy: Homepage ⇄ Service pages ⇄ Location pages, plus blog → service/homepage contextual links. Every service and location page contains 2+ natural paragraph links to the homepage with rotated anchor text.

## 4. Why These Services Were Chosen

The 30 services are the highest-intent slices of the researched USA water-damage inventory (98 keyword opportunities researched 2026-09-28), each with distinct search intent:
- **Emergency/urgency:** emergency restoration, 24/7 services — capture "near me / now" queries, the highest-value pay-per-call traffic.
- **Core process:** extraction, removal, structural drying, dehumidification, moisture detection, inspection — the terms ranking competitor pages actually use.
- **By cause:** burst/frozen pipes (Denver winter), roof/ceiling leaks (storms), plumbing/appliance/water-heater leaks, sewage/black water (highest job values) — cause-based queries convert because the searcher has already diagnosed the problem.
- **By area/property:** basement (two angles: flood events vs. any-source damage), crawl space, carpet, commercial vs. residential — matches how people describe their situation.
- **Health/finishing:** mold remediation vs. removal vs. black mold (three genuinely different intents), odor removal, sanitization — post-event concerns that extend the funnel.
Similar pages are deliberately differentiated (e.g., extraction = pulling water from materials; removal = clearing standing water; flood cleanup = cleanup phase vs. flood restoration = full cycle).

## 5. Why These Neighborhoods Were Chosen

The 30 neighborhoods are Denver's highest-population areas (population-ordered selection, mixed-year estimates — verify ZIP overlaps against Denver Open Data before treating as official). High population ≈ highest service demand density. Each page carries genuinely localized risk angles (quadrant-based, no invented landmarks/streets/stats): e.g., DIA-proximate northeast areas, basement-heavy south Denver (snowmelt seepage), older central housing (aging supply lines, phrased as general risk), Sloan Lake water-table seepage. ZIP codes are in titles/H1s for "service + ZIP" long-tail capture.

## 7. Cloudflare Pages Deployment

1. In Cloudflare dashboard: **Workers & Pages → Create → Pages → Upload assets** (or connect a git repo containing this folder).
2. Upload/point at this folder as the project root. No build command. Build output directory: `/` (root).
3. Pretty URLs work automatically: `/basement-flood-cleanup/` serves `basement-flood-cleanup/index.html`; `/404.html` is served for missing routes.
4. Verify: homepage loads, a service page, a neighborhood page, `https://rapidwaterdamagedenver.com/sitemap.xml`, `https://rapidwaterdamagedenver.com/robots.txt`.
5. DNS: point `rapidwaterdamagedenver.com` to the Pages project; enforce HTTPS (default).
