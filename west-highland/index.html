Water damage risks in West Highland

  • Frozen and burst pipes. Pipes in unheated crawlspaces, basements, garages, and exterior walls can freeze during prolonged cold and burst on the thaw, flooding rooms for hours before discovery.
  • Aging supply lines. Long-serving plumbing carries the general risks of corrosion and joint fatigue — a common pattern across Denver's older and mid-century housing.
  • Basement seepage. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain push groundwater through settled foundations and floor joints into lower levels.
  • Storm roof leaks. Hail, wind, and heavy snow exploit aging roofing and flashing, letting water into attics and wall cavities.
  • Appliance failures. Water heaters and washing machine hoses fail without warning and can release large volumes quickly.
