Water Damage Risks in Athmar Park

Summer Storm Runoff

Southwest Denver cloudbursts can drop heavy rain faster than yards and drains can absorb it. Runoff sheets across lots, pools against foundations, and pushes under doors into ground-level living spaces.

Aging Mid-Century Plumbing

As a general risk in homes of this era, original or aging supply lines, drain connections, and fixtures fatigue over the decades. Failures range from slow under-sink drips to sudden supply-line ruptures.

Roof Wear and Leaks

Years of hail seasons and UV exposure wear roofing materials. Compromised shingles and flashing let storm water into attics, where it soaks insulation and drips through ceilings.

Appliance Failures

Washing machines, dishwashers, and refrigerator water lines are everyday leak sources. A burst washer hose releases a torrent; a slow ice-maker leak rots subflooring silently.

Sewer Backups in Heavy Rain

When stormwater overwhelms the drainage system, sewage can back up through floor drains and lower-level fixtures — a contaminated mess that requires professional cleanup.

Athmar Park Water Damage FAQ

Can wet carpet and padding be saved after flooding?

It depends on the water category and response speed. Clean water extracted within hours gives carpet a chance; contaminated or long-soaked carpet and pad usually must be removed. The provider will advise on site.

Is it safe to stay in the house during restoration?

Often yes for clean-water damage in part of the home. For sewage backups or widespread mold, the provider may recommend staying elsewhere until cleanup is complete. Ask the provider about your specific situation.

How do I prevent storm runoff from entering my home?

Keep gutters and downspouts clear, extend downspouts away from the foundation, and maintain grading that slopes away from the house. These steps reduce — but can't eliminate — runoff risk during extreme storms.
