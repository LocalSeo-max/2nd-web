Water Damage Risks in University Hills

Summer Storm Runoff

Southeast Denver's intense summer downpours overwhelm yards, gutters, and street drainage. Runoff pools against foundations, floods window wells, and pushes under garage and entry doors into living spaces.

Roof and Flashing Leaks

Hail and wind-driven rain punish roofs each summer. Damaged shingles and compromised flashing around vents and chimneys let water into attics, where it saturates insulation and drips through ceilings.

Spring Snowmelt Seepage

Rapid spring thaws saturate the soil around foundations. Water finds cracks, joints, and porous spots in basement walls and slabs, dampening lower levels for weeks.

Appliance and Plumbing Leaks

Washing machine hoses, dishwasher connections, and toilet supply lines fail without warning. In ranch-style homes, a single leak can spread across a wide footprint quickly.

Sewer Backups During Downpours

Extreme rainfall can back wastewater up through lower-level drains and fixtures. Because this water is contaminated, cleanup requires professional sanitizing — not just drying.

University Hills Water Damage FAQ

How do I keep storm runoff away from my foundation?

Maintain grading that slopes away from the house, keep gutters clear, extend downspouts well away from the foundation, and avoid mulch or soil piled against the siding. During extreme storms, even good drainage can be overwhelmed — that's when fast professional help matters.

My roof is older. Should I worry about leaks every storm?

Older roofs deserve attention after hail season and major storms. Look for missing shingles, damaged flashing, and ceiling stains after rain. A provider can assess water intrusion and dry affected areas before mold sets in.

What happens if water sits for days before I notice?

Extended exposure increases the damage: drywall softens, wood swells or warps, and mold becomes likely. Providers can still help — they'll assess the full extent with moisture meters and plan extraction, drying, and any needed remediation.

Can I handle minor water damage myself?

Small, clean-water spills on hard surfaces can sometimes be handled with thorough drying. But water that reached walls, subflooring, or carpet — or any contaminated water — warrants professional equipment and expertise to prevent hidden moisture and mold.
