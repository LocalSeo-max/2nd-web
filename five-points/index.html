Water damage risks in Five Points

  • Aging plumbing systems. In housing with decades of service, supply and drain lines face the general risks of corrosion, joint failure, and fatigue — a Denver-wide concern in older building stock.
  • Burst and frozen pipes. Pipes routed through unheated spaces, crawlspaces, and exterior walls are vulnerable during extended freezes, and the resulting burst can flood multiple rooms or units.
  • Roof and flashing leaks. Summer storms and heavy snow loads can exploit worn roofing and flashing, sending water into attics and down through walls and ceilings.
  • Basement and lower-level seepage. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can push groundwater through foundation cracks and old mortar joints.
  • Sewer backups during heavy rain. Intense downpours can surcharge combined or aging sewer laterals, backing contaminated water up through lower-level drains.
