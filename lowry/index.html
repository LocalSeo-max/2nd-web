Water damage risks in Lowry

  • Appliance and water heater failures. Second-floor laundry, dishwashers, ice makers, and aging tank heaters are leading sources of sudden indoor flooding.
  • Irrigation and sprinkler breaks. Damaged sprinkler heads and lateral lines can flood yards and push water toward foundations and into lower levels.
  • Storm-driven roof leaks. Hail and high wind compromise shingles and flashing, letting water into attics and down wall cavities.
  • Drainage and grading issues. Settling soil and landscaping changes can redirect runoff toward the house, overwhelming foundation drainage during heavy rain.
  • Winter freeze damage. Hose bibs, garage pipes, and sprinkler backflow assemblies can freeze and burst during deep cold snaps.
