Water damage risks in Speer

  • Upstairs-to-downstairs leaks. A failed supply line, overflowing fixture, or broken water heater on an upper floor can soak the unit below within hours.
  • Shared plumbing stacks. Drain and supply lines serving many units see constant use, and one failure can affect multiple homes at once.
  • Water heater failures. Tank heaters in closets and utility rooms can rupture with little warning, flooding the immediate area and below.
  • Frozen pipes in winter. Pipes in exterior walls, parking levels, and unheated common areas are at risk during extended freezes.
  • Storm-driven intrusions. Heavy rain and hail can exploit roofing and window flashing, sending water into upper-floor walls and ceilings.
