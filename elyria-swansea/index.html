Water damage risks in Elyria-Swansea

  • Sewer backups during heavy rain. Intense storms can overwhelm sewer lines and laterals, pushing contaminated water back up through floor drains and basement fixtures — one of the most urgent water problems a home can face.
  • Aging supply and drain lines. Decades-old plumbing carries the general risks of corrosion, scale buildup, and joint failure, and a single failure can flood a home in hours.
  • Winter freeze damage. Pipes in crawlspaces, basements, and exterior walls can freeze and split during prolonged cold, with the burst often discovered only after the thaw.
  • Roof and flashing wear. Hail, wind, and freeze-thaw cycles degrade roofing and flashing over the years, opening paths for stormwater into attics and walls.
  • Foundation seepage. Settling foundations and old mortar can let spring snowmelt and stormwater seep into basements and lower levels.
