Water Damage Risks in Sunnyside

Aging Supply Lines

Older homes often still rely on original or aging supply plumbing. Corroded fittings, worn valves, and brittle connections can fail without warning, sending water through walls, ceilings, and floors before anyone notices.

Winter Pipe Freezes

Denver's hard freezes are tough on plumbing run through exterior walls, crawl spaces, and unheated basements. A pipe that freezes overnight can burst as it thaws, flooding a room in minutes.

Spring Snowmelt Seepage

When deep snowpack melts rapidly in spring, groundwater pressure builds around foundations. Basements and lower levels in Sunnyside can take on water through cracks, window wells, and floor joints.

Sewer Backups in Heavy Rain

Intense summer downpours can overwhelm drainage systems, pushing sewage back through floor drains and lower-level fixtures. Backup cleanup requires specialized sanitation, not just drying.

Appliance and Water Heater Failures

Washing machine hoses, dishwasher connections, and aging water heaters are common culprits. A failed water heater in a basement can release dozens of gallons before it's discovered.

Can a wet basement in Sunnyside be fully dried?
